# Evidence

The model is explicit about how strong the evidence is behind each level, so a plan can tell measured results from assumptions.

| Level | Grade | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| L1 · Foundation | Field studies | Measured in published field studies |
| L2 · Assisted | Field studies | Measured in published field studies |
| L3 · Orchestrated | Extrapolated | Extrapolated from published results and DORA performance bands |
| L4 · Autonomous with gates | Assumed | Our planning assumption; no published measurement yet |
