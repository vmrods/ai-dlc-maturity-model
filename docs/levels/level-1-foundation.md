# Level 1 · Foundation

**Prepare the program.** People do all the work. AI is used by individuals, ad hoc.

[All levels](README.md) · [L2 →](level-2-assisted.md)

## What changes at this level

- Baseline DORA and quality metrics measured
- One AI gateway: approved models, every call logged, cost per team
- Data, IP and acceptable-use policy signed off
- Champions named; pilot pods picked and trained
- Context library started: repo instructions and standards

## Exit criteria

A team reaches Level 1 when every criterion below is met with evidence.

| Dimension | Criterion | How to get there | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Governance | Executive sponsor and a charter with level targets | Write the charter and level targets, and get the sponsor's sign-off | CTO / VP Engineering |
| Governance | AI policy approved by security, legal and compliance | Draft and approve the AI policy: data in prompts, IP, acceptable use | Security, legal, compliance |
| Lifecycle | Pilot workflow defined: where AI is used and who reviews it | Define the pilot workflow: where AI drafts, who reviews, what is out of bounds | Pod leads + champions |
| Platform | All AI use through one gateway | Stand up the AI gateway: approved models, single sign-on, logging, cost per team | Platform |
| People | Pilot pods trained, each with a champion | Pick 3 pilot pods, name a champion in each and run Foundation training | Directors + AI Enablement |
| Measurement | Baseline DORA and quality metrics for every pod | Measure DORA four keys, escaped defects and rework, and run a developer survey in every pod | Engineering excellence |
| Partners | Partner teams under the same policy and gateway | Extend the AI policy and gateway access to partner and offshore teams | Vendor management + security |

## First steps

- Charter and success metrics, signed by the sponsor
- Measure the baseline: DORA four keys, escaped defects, rework, developer survey
- Stand up the AI gateway and get the AI policy approved

## Risks to watch

- Shadow AI use outside the gateway
- Skipping the baseline, so no later gain can be proven
- A policy so strict that pilots stall

## Central assets

**Documents**

- AI acceptable-use policy
- Data classification for prompts and context
- Approved tools and models register
- Program charter with level targets
- Baseline metrics report

**Shared skills**

- Repo instructions starter for pilot repos

**Training**

- Champions: Foundation (16 h)
- Pilot pods: tools, policy and reviewing AI code (4 h)

## Habits that make the move stick (to level 1)

- Name the sponsor and owners before kickoff
- Measure the baseline first: nothing can be proven without it
- Approve tools in days, not quarters
